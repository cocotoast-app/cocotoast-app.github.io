Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026 Last updated: September 22, 2026

Coco Toast is built around a simple principle: your clipboard, your mailbox and your one-time passwords stay on your own device. Our servers only handle what is needed to sell and validate a Pro licence. This policy explains exactly what that means.

1.Who we are

Coco Toast ("we", "us") develops and distributes the Coco Toast desktop application and operates its website and licence servers. For any privacy question or request you can reach us at dubiao@gmail.com.

2.Data that stays on your device

The following data is created and stored locally only. It is never transmitted to us:

  • Clipboard content. Text you copy is analysed by the desktop application on your own machine. Clipboard content is not uploaded, not logged by us and not used to build any profile.
  • E-mail account credentials. If you connect a mailbox, your IMAP password is encrypted on your device and protected by your operating system's keychain. Mail is fetched directly from your e-mail provider to your device; we are not an intermediary and we never receive your password or your mail.
  • OTP / TOTP secrets. One-time-password seeds are encrypted and stored locally in the same way. They are never uploaded.
  • Application settings (preferences, window state, language) stored in a local configuration file, plus your local Pro status file.

In short: we have no ability to read your clipboard, your e-mail or your 2FA codes. If you uninstall the application and remove its data, that information is gone.

3.Data we collect when you buy or activate a licence

To sell a licence and keep it working across your devices, our servers store the following:

Category What it contains Why
Order record Order ID, e-mail address, amount, currency, payment channel (Alipay or Paddle), payment status, order and payment timestamps. To identify your payment and issue your licence.
Licence record Licence code, e-mail address, activation limit, number of activations used, status, and whether the licence e-mail has been sent. To deliver your licence code and support the query page.
Activation record Licence code, a device hash derived from stable hardware identifiers, a device signature secret, the device information reported by the app (operating system and version, app version, machine model, CPU, memory size, architecture, hardware UUID), a session token, and activation / last-validation timestamps. To enforce the three-device limit, let you revoke a device, and keep the licence valid.
API access logs Endpoint called, timestamp, request parameters, HTTP status code and duration. Security-sensitive values such as tokens, signatures and keys are redacted before being written. Licence codes, e-mail addresses and device hashes are stored in clear text so that support and abuse investigations remain possible. To operate, troubleshoot and protect the licensing service.

We do not collect your name, postal address, phone number, government identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos or any special-category data. We do not sell any data.

4.Payments

  • USD purchases are processed by Paddle, which acts as the Merchant of Record. Paddle collects and processes your payment details and billing information as an independent controller, and issues your receipt. Please see Paddle's own privacy policy for how it handles that data.
  • CNY purchases are processed by Alipay. Your payment is handled inside Alipay's own environment, governed by Alipay's privacy policy.
  • We never see or store your full card number, CVV or banking credentials. We receive only the resulting order reference, a masked or abbreviated e-mail address where applicable, the amount and the payment status.
  • When you open a checkout, the payment provider's own scripts are loaded from its servers and may set cookies or collect device signals for fraud prevention, under its own policies.

5.Cookies and local storage on the website

  • Our website does not use advertising cookies and does not run third-party analytics or tracking scripts.
  • We store your language preference in your browser's localStorage, and a short-lived navigation value in sessionStorage so that opening a page directly from a link works correctly. Both stay in your browser and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.
  • Third parties whose services you reach from our site (Paddle, Alipay, GitHub) may set their own cookies under their own policies.

6.E-mail we send you

We send transactional e-mail only: your licence code after a successful purchase, the result of a licence query you requested, and replies to support requests. We may use a third-party transactional e-mail delivery service to send these messages, which receives your e-mail address and the message content for that purpose. We do not send newsletters or marketing e-mail.

7.Service providers and disclosures

We share personal data only with:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that run our servers, database and static website;
  • Payment providers (Paddle for USD, Alipay for CNY) to process your purchase and handle refunds;
  • Transactional e-mail delivery services used to send your licence code;
  • Authorities, where we are legally required to disclose data, or where disclosure is necessary to investigate fraud or protect our rights.

These providers act under contract and are permitted to use the data only to provide their service to us. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

8.International transfers

Our infrastructure and the service providers listed above may be located outside your country. Where required, transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. By purchasing a licence you understand that your order and licence data may be processed in another jurisdiction.

9.How long we keep data

  • Order, licence and activation records are kept while your licence is active and for as long as we are required to keep accounting and tax records afterwards. You can ask us to delete them once your licence is no longer needed — see section 11 — but we may have to retain limited records for legal reasons.
  • API access logs are kept for a limited period for troubleshooting and abuse prevention, and are then deleted.
  • Data on your device is under your control; deleting the application's data or using the in-app "clear all data" option removes it.

10.Security

We protect data in transit with TLS, restrict server-side access to what is needed to operate the service, redact tokens, signatures and keys before writing logs, and store account passwords and OTP secrets only in encrypted form on your own device. No system is perfectly secure, but we design the service so that the most sensitive data — your clipboard, your e-mail and your 2FA secrets — never reaches us in the first place.

11.Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to receive it in a portable format, or to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, e-mail dubiao@gmail.com from the address you used to purchase. We will respond within the period required by applicable law (normally 30 days) and may ask you to verify that the e-mail address belongs to you.

Our legal bases for processing are the performance of our contract with you (issuing and validating your licence, providing support), our legitimate interests (preventing fraud and abuse, securing the service) and compliance with legal obligations (tax and accounting records).

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. We do not sell personal information, and we do not knowingly process the data of children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the applicable age of consent).

12.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the service or to the law. The "Last updated" date at the top will change accordingly, and material changes will be announced on our website. Continuing to use the Software after an update means you accept the revised policy.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 22 日 最后更新：2026 年 9 月 22 日

Coco Toast 遵循一个简单原则：你的剪贴板、邮箱与一次性密码始终留在你自己的设备上。 我们的服务器只处理「卖出一份 Pro 授权并让它持续可用」所必需的信息。本政策逐项说明这件事。

1.我们是谁

Coco Toast（下称「我们」）开发并发行 Coco Toast 桌面应用，并运营其网站与授权服务器。 任何隐私相关的问题或请求，均可通过 dubiao@gmail.com 联系我们。

2.仅保存在你本机的数据

以下数据仅在你本机生成和保存，绝不会传输给我们：

  • 剪贴板内容。你复制的内容由桌面应用在你本机分析处理， 不上传、我们也不记录，更不会用于建立任何画像。
  • 邮箱账户凭据。若你接入邮箱，IMAP 密码会在本机加密， 并由操作系统钥匙串保护。邮件由你的邮箱服务商直接拉取到你的设备， 我们不作为中间人，也绝不会收到你的密码或邮件内容。
  • OTP / TOTP 密钥。一次性密码密钥以同样的方式加密后仅存于本机， 不会上传。
  • 应用设置（偏好项、窗口状态、语言）保存在本机配置文件中， 以及本机的 Pro 授权状态文件。

简而言之：我们无法读取你的剪贴板、邮件或两步验证码。 卸载应用并清除其数据后，这些信息即被删除。

3.购买或激活授权时我们收集的数据

为完成授权销售并让它在你的多台设备上持续可用，我们的服务器会保存以下信息：

类别 包含内容 用途
订单记录 订单号、邮箱地址、金额、币种、支付渠道（支付宝或 Paddle）、 支付状态、下单与支付时间。 确认你的付款并签发授权。
授权记录 激活码、邮箱地址、可激活设备数上限、已使用次数、授权状态， 以及授权邮件是否已发送。 投递激活码，并支撑查询页功能。
激活记录 激活码、由稳定硬件标识派生的设备哈希、设备签名用密钥、 应用上报的设备信息（操作系统及版本、应用版本、机型、CPU、内存大小、 架构、硬件 UUID）、会话令牌，以及激活时间与最近校验时间。 执行三台设备上限、支持你撤销设备、维持授权有效。
接口调用日志 调用的接口、调用时间、请求参数、HTTP 状态码与耗时。 令牌、签名、密钥等敏感值会在写入前脱敏。 激活码、邮箱地址与设备哈希为明文保存，以便必要时进行客服支持与滥用调查。 运维、排障与保护授权服务。

我们不会收集你的姓名、通讯地址、电话号码、身份证件号码、精确定位、 通讯录、照片或任何特殊类别个人信息，也不会出售任何数据。

4.支付

  • 美元订单由 Paddle 处理，Paddle 在这些交易中作为记录商户 （Merchant of Record），以其独立控制者身份收集与处理你的付款信息和账单信息， 并出具收据。其数据处理方式请见 Paddle 的隐私政策。
  • 人民币订单由支付宝处理。支付在支付宝自有环境内完成， 适用支付宝的隐私政策。
  • 我们不会看到、也不会存储你的完整卡号、CVV 或银行凭据， 只会收到订单号、适用的脱敏或缩写邮箱、金额与支付状态。
  • 打开收银台时，支付渠道自身的脚本会从其服务器加载， 可能为风控目的设置 Cookie 或采集设备信息，适用其各自的政策。

5.网站上的 Cookie 与本地存储

  • 我们的网站不使用广告 Cookie，也不加载第三方统计 或追踪脚本。
  • 我们会在浏览器 localStorage 中保存你的语言偏好， 并在 sessionStorage 中保存一个用于页面直达跳转的临时值 （使从链接直接打开某个页面能正常工作）。两者都只保存在你的浏览器里， 可随时通过浏览器设置清除。
  • 你从我站跳转使用的第三方服务（Paddle、支付宝、GitHub）可能按其自身政策设置 Cookie。

6.我们发给你的邮件

我们只发送事务性邮件：购买成功后的激活码、你主动发起的激活码查询结果， 以及对技术支持请求的回复。我们可能使用第三方事务性邮件投递服务发送这些邮件， 该服务为此目的会接收你的邮箱地址与邮件内容。我们不发送订阅邮件，也不发送营销邮件。

7.服务商与信息披露

我们仅在下列情形下共享个人信息：

  • 托管与基础设施服务商，用于运行我们的服务器、数据库与静态网站；
  • 支付渠道（美元为 Paddle、人民币为支付宝）， 用于完成付款与处理退款；
  • 事务性邮件投递服务，用于发送激活码；

上述服务商依合同约定行事，仅可为向我们提供服务之目的使用数据。 我们不出售、出租或交易个人信息，也不会将其用于广告投放。

9.数据保留期限

  • 订单、授权与激活记录在授权有效期内保留； 授权不再需要后，如法律要求（会计与税务留存）我们仍会保留一段时间的有限记录， 其余部分可应你要求删除（见第 11 条）。

10.安全措施

我们使用 TLS 保护传输中的数据，将服务器侧访问权限限制在运维所必需的范围内， 在写入日志前对令牌、签名与密钥脱敏，账户密码与 OTP 密钥仅以加密形式存放在你自己的设备上。 没有绝对安全的系统，但我们的设计目标是：最敏感的数据—— 你的剪贴板、邮件与两步验证密钥——从一开始就不会到达我们这里。
