Refund Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026 Last updated: September 22, 2026

We want you to be happy with Coco Toast Pro. If it is not right for you, you can request a refund within 14 days of purchase. Because the licence is delivered instantly and payment processing charges are not returned to us, the refunded amount is the price you paid less any payment-platform fees and non-refundable taxes.

1.Scope

This policy applies to all purchases of a Coco Toast Pro licence, whether made in CNY through Alipay or in USD through Paddle. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

2.The 14-day refund window

  • You may request a refund within 14 calendar days of the date your payment was completed. The payment date is shown in your payment receipt and in the order lookup on our website.
  • Requests received after the 14-day window are normally declined. If you are outside the window because of a genuine problem — for example the software did not work on your device and we could not fix it — write to us anyway and we will review it in good faith.
  • You do not need to give a reason, but telling us what went wrong helps us improve the product.

3.How much is refunded

The refunded amount is calculated as follows:

Refund = amount you paid − payment-platform processing fees − non-refundable taxes

In practice this means:

  • Payment-platform fees. Alipay and Paddle charge a processing fee on each transaction, and that fee is generally not returned to us when a transaction is reversed. This fee is deducted from the refund.
  • Taxes and duties. Any VAT, GST, sales tax or similar tax collected at checkout and not recoverable by us is deducted as well.
  • Currency and transfer costs. If the payment involved a currency conversion or an international transfer, the resulting conversion or transfer cost may also be deducted.
  • We do not charge any additional administration or handling fee of our own on top of these deductions.

Because the deductions depend on the payment channel, the currency and your billing country, we cannot state a fixed percentage in advance. We will tell you the exact refund amount in writing when we confirm your request, before the refund is processed. If you do not agree with the amount, you are free to cancel the request.

4.How to request a refund

  1. E-mail dubiao@gmail.com from the address you used at checkout.
  2. Include your order ID (or the licence code) and, if you have it, your payment receipt or transaction reference.
  3. Optional: tell us briefly why you are requesting the refund.

We will acknowledge your request within 3 business days and confirm the exact refund amount. Once you agree, the refund is issued to the original payment method:

Purchase channel Who processes the refund Typical time to arrive
CNY via Alipay We initiate the refund through Alipay. Usually within a few business days.
USD via Paddle Paddle, as Merchant of Record, processes the refund to your original payment method. Usually within 5–15 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

The time for the money to appear on your statement is controlled by the payment provider and your bank, and is outside our control.

5.What happens to your licence

  • Once a refund is issued, the associated licence code is deactivated, together with all devices activated with it. The application returns to its free feature set.
  • You do not need to uninstall the software, and your local data — clipboard history processed locally, mailbox accounts and OTP entries — stays on your device and remains yours.
  • If you later decide to buy again, you can do so at any time and will receive a new licence code.

6.When a refund is not available

We may decline a refund where:

  • the request is made more than 14 days after the payment date;
  • the licence was obtained fraudulently, with a stolen payment method, or is the subject of an abusive chargeback;
  • the licence code has been resold, shared publicly or transferred in breach of the Terms of Service;
  • the licence has already been refunded or reversed for the same order;
  • the request relates to an incompatible device or operating system that was clearly stated as unsupported on our website at the time of purchase.

This list is not exhaustive, but we will always explain the reason if we decline a request.

7.Chargebacks and payment disputes

Please contact us before opening a chargeback or payment dispute — we can almost always resolve the matter faster directly. A chargeback without prior contact will cause the licence to be deactivated, and we may suspend the ability to purchase future licences from the same e-mail address or payment method.

8.Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits or excludes any mandatory consumer right that applies to you under the law of your country or region of residence, including any statutory right of withdrawal or any right to a remedy for a defective product. Where such a right gives you more than this policy does, that right prevails.

9.Contact

Refund requests and questions: e-mail dubiao@gmail.com. We usually reply within 3 business days.

退款政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 22 日 最后更新：2026 年 9 月 22 日

我们希望你对 Coco Toast Pro 感到满意。如果它不适合你，可以在购买后 14 天内申请退款。 由于授权是即时交付的，且支付渠道收取的手续费不会退还给我们， 实际退款金额为你实付金额扣除付款平台手续费与不可退还的税费后的部分。

1.适用范围

本政策适用于所有 Coco Toast Pro 授权的购买，无论是通过支付宝以人民币支付， 还是通过 Paddle 以美元支付。本政策是 服务条款的组成部分。

2.14 天退款窗口

  • 你可以在付款完成之日起 14 个自然日内申请退款。 付款日期可在你的支付凭证，以及我站的订单查询结果中看到。
  • 超过 14 天收到的申请通常不予受理。但若你是因真实问题而超期 （例如软件在你的设备上无法使用，而我们也没能帮你解决）， 仍可来信说明，我们会善意地重新评估。
  • 申请退款无需说明理由，不过如果你愿意告知问题所在，会帮助我们改进产品。

3.退款金额如何计算

退款金额的计算方式如下：

退款金额 = 你实付金额 − 支付平台手续费 − 不可退还的税费

具体来说：

  • 支付平台手续费。支付宝与 Paddle 都会对每笔交易收取手续费， 交易被撤销时这部分费用通常不会退还给我们，因此会从退款金额中扣除。
  • 税费。结算时收取、且我们无法退回的增值税、消费税或类似税费， 同样会从中扣除。
  • 货币与转账成本。若付款过程涉及货币兑换或跨境转账， 由此产生的兑换或转账成本也可能被扣除。
  • 在上述扣除之外，我们不会额外收取任何手续费或管理费。

由于扣除项取决于支付渠道、币种与你所在账单国家/地区，我们无法事先给出固定比例。 我们会在确认你的申请时、退款处理之前，以书面形式告知准确退款金额； 如果你不接受该金额，可以随时取消此次退款申请。

4.如何申请退款

  1. 请使用下单时的邮箱，致信 dubiao@gmail.com。
  2. 请在邮件中提供订单号（或激活码），如有支付凭证或交易流水号也请一并附上。
  3. 可选：简要说明申请退款的原因。

我们会在 3 个工作日内回复并确认准确的退款金额。 你确认后，退款将退回至原支付方式：

购买渠道 退款由谁处理 到账大致时间
支付宝（人民币） 由我们通过支付宝发起退款。 通常在数个工作日内到账。
Paddle（美元） 由 Paddle 作为记录商户（Merchant of Record）退款至你的原支付方式。 通常 5–15 个工作日，视你的银行或发卡机构而定。

款项显示在账单上的具体时间由支付渠道与你的银行决定，不在我们的控制范围内。

5.退款后授权会怎样

  • 退款完成后，对应的激活码会被停用，使用该激活码激活的设备一并失效， 应用将回到免费功能集。
  • 你无需卸载软件；本机数据（本机处理的剪贴板记录、邮箱账户、OTP 条目） 仍保留在你的设备上，也仍然属于你。
  • 如果日后你决定再次购买，随时可以下单，我们会签发新的激活码。

6.不予退款的情形

以下情形我们可能拒绝退款：

  • 申请时间超过付款日之后 14 天；
  • 授权系以欺诈方式、使用盗取的支付方式取得，或涉及恶意拒付；
  • 激活码被转售、公开发布，或以违反服务条款的方式转让；
  • 同一订单已经完成过退款或被撤销；
  • 申请所涉问题为设备或操作系统不兼容，而该不兼容情况在购买时已在我站明确标注为不支持。

上述情形并非穷举；如我们拒绝退款申请，一定会向你说明原因。

7.拒付与支付争议

在发起拒付（chargeback）或支付争议之前，请先与我们联系——直接沟通几乎总能更快解决。 未事先沟通的拒付将导致授权被停用，我们也可能限制同一邮箱或同一支付方式日后再次购买。

8.你的法定权利

本政策不排除或限制你依所在国家/地区法律享有的、不可放弃的消费者法定权利， 包括法定撤回权，以及针对瑕疵产品的救济权利。 若此类法定权利给予你的保障优于本政策，则以该法定权利为准。

9.联系方式

退款申请与相关咨询：请致信 dubiao@gmail.com，我们通常在 3 个工作日内回复。
